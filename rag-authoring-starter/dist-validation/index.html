DASH-IF Interoperability Guidelines, Version 5 (IOP V5)

IOP V5 is a multi-part set of documents. Version 5 is defined for use with MPEG-DASH (ISO/IEC 23009-1) and constrained to deliver media formatted according to MPEG CMAF (ISO/IEC 23000-19). This page is generated from the specification sources; parts marked under development are not yet built here.

part01-overview (under development)Overview, architecture and interfaces: reference architecture, interfaces and functional blocks.
part02-core-cmaf (under development)Core principles and CMAF mapping: DASH data and timing model and the mapping of CMAF to DASH.
part03-on-demand (under development)On-demand services: requirements and recommendations for DASH on-demand.
part04-live-low-latency (under development)Live and low-latency live services: live service offerings including low-latency.
part05-ad-insertion (under development)Ad insertion: SSAI and SGAI in a CMAF/DASH workflow.
part06-content-protection (under development)Content protection and security: CENC, key rotation, ECCP and the content-protection schema.
part07-video (under development)Video: CMAF media profiles and DASH signalling for video tracks.
part08-audio (under development)Audio: audio interoperability points, coding profiles, packaging and MPD parameters.
part09-text (under development)Text: subtitle and caption interoperability points and signalling.
part10-events (under development)Events: MPD and inband events and timed metadata tracks.
part11-additional-technologies (under development)Additional functionalities: thumbnails, query/token mechanisms, metadata tracks.
